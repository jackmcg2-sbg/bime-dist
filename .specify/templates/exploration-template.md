## Multi-agent rules *(read before writing anything)*

Several different LLMs (or several sessions of the same one) may explore
concurrently. The file, not any one conversation, is the working memory
(state-externalization, after the Research Harness / Harness-1 design).

1. **Append-only** everywhere except **Synthesis** and **Decision**. Never
   rewrite, renumber, or delete another agent's entries — including refuted
   ones (mark them, keep them).
2. **Agent identity rule**: every entry carries `Agent` (product/model, e.g.
   `claude-fable-5`, `codex`, `gemini-cli`) and a UTC timestamp.
3. **Entry IDs are globally unique within the file**: next free integer per
   table (`M#`, `E#`, `V#`, `O#`). On collision from a concurrent append,
   re-read the file and take the next free ID before saving.
4. **Synthesis is regenerated, not appended**: any agent may rewrite it
   whole, but must (a) state its identity, UTC time, and the highest entry ID
   incorporated, and (b) carry forward, not drop, contradictions it cannot
   resolve.
5. **Dedup, don't repeat**: before a search/inspection, scan the Observations
   log; a repeat is recorded as `dup-of:O#` and costs the marginal-gain
   counter (see Stop rule).
6. **Decision is human-only**: agents fill everything above it; only the
   human sets Status to go/no-go/parked and signs the Decision.

## Stop rule

3 consecutive actions that add no new live evidence (marginal gain exhausted),
or on an explicit human park/no-go. Before stopping, regenerate Synthesis.
